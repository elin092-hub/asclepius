Asclepius

The math-powered load tracker that tells you when to push, when to ease off, and when to rest — before an injury beats you to it.

What Asclepius does

Asclepius utilizes math in conjunction with ACWR (Acute-To-Chronic Workload Ratio) to advise athletes — with no cost — on whether their day of training is likely to cause injury, perhaps advising that they take a day to rest.

By applying an EWMA model, Asclepius weighs recent training more heavily and applies a natural decay to older sessions, generating an accurate risk profile from the very first workout. It can be used immediately — no 28-day wait.

This model is more realistic to how the human body actually experiences fatigue: a tiring workout from three weeks ago isn’t the same as a strenuous session today.

What the numbers mean

< 0.8Undertraining — you could be doing more.
0.8 – 1.3The safe range — keep on keeping on.
1.3 – 1.5Elevated — proceed with caution.
≥ 1.5High risk — seriously consider a rest day.

Try it yourself

Log a session and watch the ACWR respond instantly. A marathon-grade effort — say, 360 minutes at intensity 8 — will push you straight into the red. Lay off the next day and you’ll watch the ratio climb back down.

Asclepius is a service that utilizes math and the biological sciences to prevent athletic injuries. It pushes towards the democratization of elite sports science, ensuring that every student athlete — regardless of family income — has the tools to train safely, stay healthy, and stay in the game.
